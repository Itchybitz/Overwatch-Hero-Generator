About Overwatch Slots

Overwatch Slots started as a side project for my friends and me — a way to settle who plays what in Overwatch without the argument. They asked me to publish it, so here we are.

Enter your squad, hit SPIN, and the slots deal every player a role and a hero. No more fighting over who has to tank.

How it works

  • Pick a mode — 5v5 (1‑2‑2), 6v6 (2‑2‑2), or a solo spin. Cross out roles per player and ban heroes from the pool to shape fate.
  • Every spin deals a legal comp plus minor and major perks. Rerolling a hero always costs a 😈 punishment dare.
  • Turn on Dares and every player gets one; about 1 in 4 spins adds a team-wide dare on top.
  • Write your own House Dares — they're saved in your browser and jump the queue when the slots roll.
  • About 1 in 20 spins goes ✨ golden: a guaranteed team dare, longer reels, and one player's card turns 🃏 WILD — they may pick any hero of their role, even a banned one.
  • Tonight logs every spin of the session; flip "no repeats tonight" and nobody replays a hero.
  • Share link replays an exact team, Copy as image makes a Discord-ready picture, and Share this setup hands your squad, bans, and house dares to a friend in one link.
